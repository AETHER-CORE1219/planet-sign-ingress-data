Planet sign ingress data

Open data: every time the Moon (tropical and sidereal/Lahiri), Venus, Mars and Mercury entered a new zodiac sign from 1940 to 2030, in UTC and JST. Free under CC BY 4.0.

  • Venus, Mars, Mercury (3,130 rows): CSV · JSON
  • Moon (14,599 tropical + 14,598 sidereal rows): CSV · JSON
